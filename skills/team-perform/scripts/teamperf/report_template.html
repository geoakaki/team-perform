    How the scores are made

    • Rule scores come from scripts: messages over every commit that isn't a merge in the window; size, tests, hygiene and security from each person's last such commits and their diffs.
    • Review scores (when present) come from a reviewer reading the same sample and judging design, correctness and risk. Rules cannot see those.
    • Identities are merged by login, email and author name, plus the aliases in the config.
    • A short sample is not a performance review. Squash-merge workflows and single large features can look worse than they are.
